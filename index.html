What we do

We make games.
That’s it.

Seriously, that’s the whole plan. We build Roblox games we love playing, keep them fresh with updates, and try to grow a little every day.

01

Games from scratch

Every game starts as a random idea — like “what if escaping a giant keyboard with a jetpack was a thing?” Then we build it, test it, and hit publish.

02

Updates, always

We play our own games way too much. When something feels off, or players ask for something, we start cooking the next update.

03

Growing the honest way

No magic tricks here. Just fun games, word of mouth, and players who keep coming back. Step by step, that’s how a small studio grows.
